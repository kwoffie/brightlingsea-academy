Big futures start with small, brave steps.

Brightlingsea Academy is a community day school in Kumasi where children from Nursery through Junior High learn, play and grow together. Our classes are small, our teachers know every child by name, and our gates are open to every family.

We keep lessons practical, discipline kind and standards high — so pupils leave us confident, curious and ready for BECE and beyond.

Our Story

Brightlingsea Academy opened its doors on 9 October 2016 as a small child care centre serving just 20 children and five staff members. A decade later, the Academy is home to over 400 pupils and a dedicated team of qualified teachers, and is recognised for the quality of education it offers to the families of Asennua and the wider Kwabre East community.

We believe that every child has unique potential. Our holistic approach combines rigorous academics with extracurricular activities, character development, and moral instruction — preparing students not just for BECE, but for life.
