Our Anniversary

Celebrating a decade of excellence, community impact and unforgettable memories — 2016 to 2026.

OFFICIAL SCHOOL ANNIVERSARY
10
Years of Excellence
Empowering Minds, Transforming Lives.

A Legacy of Dedication

Brightlingsea Academy has dedicated itself to one purpose: bringing hope, changing lives and creating a brighter future for underprivileged children through quality education. From 20 children and five staff in our first week to over 400 pupils today, our growth has been driven by an unwavering commitment to the children we serve.

This anniversary page celebrates the milestones, achievements and memories that have shaped our journey — and honours the parents, teachers, supporters and community members who made it all possible.

HISTORY

Our Journey Through Time

2009

A Friendship Begins

Mrs. Jemma Williams, a British national, and Dr. Ben Antwi-Agyei Boateng, a Ghanaian, meet as staff members at Roman Girls Junior High School, Ash-Town. Their shared vision grows into an educational initiative that would change many children’s lives.

9 Oct 2016

Brightlingsea Child Care Centre Opens

The school is established and named after Brightlingsea, the town in the United Kingdom where Mrs. Jemma comes from, in recognition of the support and love shown by her family and townsfolk. The opening is attended by the chief and people of Asennua, and Pastor Albert Antwi Boasiako of the Seventh-day Adventist Church dedicates the school.

10 Oct 2016

The First Pupils Register

Registration commences. The school opens with five staff members and enrols 20 children during its first week, in surroundings that were still largely undeveloped.

2017

Renamed Brightlingsea Academy

The institution is renamed Brightlingsea Academy as it expands to address the academic, social, economic and spiritual needs of the families it serves. British teacher Peter Harvey teaches the pupils for several weeks and helps with the building work.

2018

Ten British Sponsors Visit

Ten British sponsors, including Mrs. Williams’ parents, visit the school. Their visit includes an educational field trip to Lake Bosomtwe, which becomes a memorable experience for the pupils.

2020

Home Learning Intervention

The COVID-19 pandemic closes schools nationwide. In response, the Academy introduces a Home Learning intervention through which staff visit pupils at designated community centres to keep learning going.

2023

7th Anniversary & JHS Inaugurated

The Academy marks its seventh anniversary and inaugurates its Junior High School department, celebrated with representatives of the Childrensalon team from the United Kingdom and officials of ARAMEX Ghana.

2024

Boarding House Secured

The Academy secures a building to accommodate final-year pupils under its boarding system, together with residential accommodation for staff — strengthening support for the pioneer JHS cohort ahead of the BECE.

2026

100% BECE Placement

All 19 BECE candidates qualify for placement, receive boarding status and gain admission to their preferred programmes of study at senior high school level.

Oct 2026

10th Anniversary Celebration

Brightlingsea Academy celebrates a decade of excellence under the theme “Empowering Minds, Transforming Lives: Celebrating a Decade of Excellence through Quality Education.”

Anniversary Memories

These moments capture the heart of our anniversary celebration — each photo tells a story of community, achievement, and shared joy.

400+ Pupils

From Nursery 1 through to JHS 3, we are home to more than 400 pupils — all of them learning, growing and preparing to become responsible contributors to Ghana and the world.

100% BECE Pass Rate

Every one of our candidates qualified for placement, received boarding status and gained admission to their preferred senior high school programmes.
