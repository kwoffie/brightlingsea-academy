School Activities

Sports, clubs, events, and everything that makes life at Brightlingsea Academy vibrant and fun.

More Than Just Academics

At Brightlingsea Academy, we believe in developing the whole child. Our wide range of activities — from sports and clubs to community service and annual events — helps students discover their talents, build confidence, and develop leadership skills.

Sports

Athletics & Track Events

Our athletes compete in the annual inter-school athletics competition, consistently winning medals in sprints, relays, and long-distance events.

Football
Sports

Football Team

Our boys' and girls' football teams train twice a week and compete in regional tournaments. The team has won the district championship 3 years in a row.

Whole School
Annual Events

Sports Day

Our annual inter-house sports day is the highlight of the year. From sprints to sack races, every student participates. Parents are invited to cheer.

Whole School
Annual Events

Cultural Day Celebration

Students showcase the rich cultural diversity of Ghana through traditional dress, food, music, dance, and storytelling. A vibrant celebration of our heritage.

Volunteering
Community Service

School Garden Project

Students learn about agriculture and sustainability by maintaining a school garden. Produce is used in the cafeteria and sometimes donated to a local orphanage.
