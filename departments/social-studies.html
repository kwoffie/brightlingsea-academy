Social Studies & Ghanaian Languages Department

Academic programs, curriculum, achievements, and staff.

Academic Excellence in Social Studies & Ghanaian Languages

Part of Brightlingsea Academy's comprehensive Junior High School program

Back to All Departments

The Social Studies department builds citizenship, cultural awareness, and critical understanding of society. Students study economics, geography, history, and Ghanaian languages, developing a well-rounded understanding of their environment and heritage.

Curriculum by Level
JHS 1
Our environment, map work, Ghanaian culture, basic economics, local history
JHS 2
West African geography, economic activities, governance, conflict resolution, Ghanaian language basics
JHS 3
BECE social studies syllabus, population studies, trade, citizenship, Ghanaian language proficiency
Our Achievements
  • Students consistently perform well in BECE Social Studies (94% pass rate)
  • School debate team coached by department wins regional championships
  • Students participate in national essay competitions on Ghanaian history and culture
