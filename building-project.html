OUR COMMUNITY BRIDGE

A Bridge Built for the Neighbourhood

Beyond our own walls, we are building a footbridge for the community — a contribution of the school to its surroundings, in appreciation of their support and for our mutual coexistence.

Brightlingsea Academy has grown because the people around us believed in us. Parents gave their time, neighbours lent tools and advice, local builders carried our materials, and the assembly gave us the land we stand on. The bridge is our way of saying thank you in the most practical form we know how.

It will give families, traders and schoolchildren a safe, direct route across the busy access road — shortening a journey that is currently long and risky, especially for the children who walk to school every morning. Local labour has been used wherever possible, and the design was agreed in open community consultation with the elders, the parents' association and the district assembly before the first block was laid.

When it is completed, the bridge will be handed over to the community with a written maintenance arrangement, so that what we build together is cared for together.
