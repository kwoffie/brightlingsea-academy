Our History

Building Future Leaders Since 2016

The establishment of Brightlingsea Academy was inspired by the longstanding friendship between Mrs. Jemma Williams, a British national, and Dr. Ben Antwi-Agyei Boateng, a Ghanaian, who first met in 2009 as staff members at Roman Girls Junior High School, Ash-Town. Their shared vision and sustained communication eventually developed into an educational initiative that has positively impacted many children and families.

Brightlingsea was established on the 9th of October 2016, initially as “Brightlingsea Child Care Centre.” The name ‘Brightlingsea’ is a town in the United Kingdom where Mrs. Jemma comes from. The name was adopted for the school in recognition of the immense support and the great show of love shown by the family and townsfolk of Mrs. Jemma.

The opening ceremony was attended by the chief and people of Asennua, the assembly member, opinion leaders, family members and the community. Pastor Albert Antwi Boasiako of the Seventh-day Adventist Church dedicated the school to the care of the Lord. Mrs. Williams also spent several weeks in Ghana to support the realisation of the project.

Registration of pupils commenced on Monday, 10th October 2016. The school began with five staff members and enrolled 20 children during its first week. At the time, the surrounding communities were still developing and much of the immediate environment remained undeveloped.

In 2017, the institution was renamed Brightlingsea Academy as it expanded to address the academic, social, economic and spiritual needs of the families it served.

The Academy has benefited from visits and support from several individuals from the United Kingdom. Among them was Peter Harvey, a British teacher who taught the pupils for several weeks and contributed to the construction of the school building by assisting with the movement of blocks to the first floor of the old building. Other visitors included Mr. Steve Williams, Laura Collins, Eddy, Maxwell, Dr. Philip Tibbs, Gregg, Vandi and Becky, Mark Smith, Lia Smith, Georgie, Berthany, Mrs. Gracie Browning, Asher, David and Emma J.

What Our Children Receive

Every child at Brightlingsea Academy benefits from a complete package of care and support — because a child who is well fed, healthy and safe learns best.

Free education — no fees for the families we serve.
Two nutritious meals a day — cooked fresh on campus.
Healthy snacks throughout the week.
Free school uniforms — provided to every pupil.
Medical cards covering access to doctors and reduced-price prescriptions.
Vaccinations and medical checks including opticians.
School bus transporting children who live further away into school.
Home social worker visits and support with food and items needed for school.

Our Core Values

These values guide everything we do — from how we teach, to how we interact with parents and the community.

Academic Excellence — We set high standards and support every student to achieve them.
Integrity & Discipline — We teach honesty, respect, and self-discipline as core life skills.
Innovation — We embrace new teaching methods, technology, and creative thinking.
Community Service — We encourage students to give back and be active citizens.
Inclusivity — Every child is welcome, regardless of background. We celebrate diversity.
Continuous Improvement — We constantly reflect and improve our teaching and facilities.
