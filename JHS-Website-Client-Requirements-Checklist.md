# JHS Website - Client Requirements Checklist

### **Purpose**
This checklist is designed to ensure we gather everything needed from the school upfront — saving weeks of back-and-forth during development. Please go through each section and provide the requested items before we begin design.

  - [ ] School events / celebrations (past years) — for Anniversary page

- [ ] **Videos** (optional but impactful):
